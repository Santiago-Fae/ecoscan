Why recycle?

🌍 Less landfill waste

Much of what we throw away can go back into the production chain, reducing the volume sent to landfills.

⚡ Saves resources

Recycling aluminum, for example, takes far less energy than producing it from ore.

🤝 Creates income

The recycling chain supports cooperatives and thousands of waste pickers. Sorting correctly helps them directly.

Sort in 3 steps

  1. Clean: rinse packaging, without wasting water.
  2. Separate: each material in its own bin or collection point.
  3. Drop off: use selective collection, a recycling center or a cooperative.
